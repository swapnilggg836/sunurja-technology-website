Privacy Policy

How Sunurja Technology collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Last Updated: November 2026

Note: This Privacy Policy is a standard draft and should be reviewed by the business owner or legal counsel before commercial launch.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Sunurja Technology ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to respecting your privacy and protecting any personal information you share with us while browsing our website or requesting solar energy consultations, product information, or installation services.

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website, we may collect the following details voluntarily submitted by you:

  • Enquiry Form Details: Full Name, Email Address, Phone Number, Property Type (Residential, Commercial, or Industrial), Solar Product/Service Interest, and your custom message.
  • Newsletter Subscriptions: Your Email Address when you sign up for solar updates and energy-saving tips.
  • Technical & Usage Data: Basic browser type, device information, and pages visited to improve website performance and user experience.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected strictly for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Responding to your solar consultation requests and product enquiries.
  • Preparing customized solar system recommendations and site assessment schedules.
  • Sending requested newsletters, solar news, and maintenance tips (you may opt out at any time).
  • Improving our website functionality, security, and customer support.

4. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may use trusted third-party tools—such as form submission processors (e.g., Formspree), email delivery providers, or web hosting services—solely to operate our website and respond to your enquiries securely.

5. Data Security & Retention

We implement reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. We retain your contact details only as long as necessary to fulfill your enquiry or comply with applicable legal obligations.

6. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal data you have submitted to us. To exercise these rights or unsubscribe from communications, please reach out using the contact details below.
